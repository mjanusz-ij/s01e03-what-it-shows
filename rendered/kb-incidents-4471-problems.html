38 of 412 reports carry an intake tag that is not their problem.

  1. parser: the shared parser reads dates month first. Every tenant in #4471 writes the day first, so 7 April comes out as 4 July. Every tenant, every channel.
  2. hint: the booking-v3 form tells people to type month first. It prints MM/DD/YYYY, then reads the field day first, the tenant’s way. Only form reports from the three booking-v3 tenants. A correct parser does not fix a wrong hint.
  3. shift: dates move by one day. Not a swap: Tuesday becomes Monday, one meeting lands on different days in Lisbon and Toronto, every booking in June is one day off. A time zone bug. The desk had marked these reports in desk-notes.json but still counted them under the parser.

How this was counted

  • One id, one report: the export pages overlap, so some reports come twice.
  • The cause comes from the tenant (its locale and its booking form, causes.ts), not from the intake tag. Intake guessed from the reporter’s browser, and the guess is often wrong.
  • Counted the desk’s way: each report under the cause its tenant gives it. A one-day move is also its own problem, so those reports count under parser and again under shift. Their ids come from the desk’s notes and match the only report bodies that describe one.
  • Decision 0001 merges on subject line. These reports use twelve subjects across all three problems, so the subject says nothing about the cause.
  • Build with full locale data. Without it, no locale reads as day-first and every report is unplaced.
